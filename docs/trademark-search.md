# Trademark first-look – "Prepeat" / "Prep+Eat"

Searched 2026-07-27 by Claude. **This is a first look, not a clearance
search, and not legal advice.** TMview is explicitly not an official register.
It does not cover unregistered rights, company names, or pending applications
not yet published. Before spending money on a registration, or before a US or
UK launch, put this in front of a trademark attorney.

Relevant classes for an app like this: **9** (downloadable software) and
**42** (software as a service). Class 43 is food services, 21 is kitchen
containers, 32 is drinks – neighbours, not overlaps.

## Verdict

**The NAME looks clear for Denmark and the EU. The TAGLINE does not.**

Nobody holds "Prepeat" as a trademark anywhere. The exposures are a
near-identical competitor in English-speaking markets, and a tagline that two
other parties have already registered – one of them in the exact class.

Not on the App Store either. Two uses of the name in the wild, neither
registered:

- **Google Play "Prepeat"** (`com.prepeat.user`) – a chef-prepared meal and
  tiffin delivery service. Food sector, so prior use worth knowing about, but
  no trademark and no sign of a Danish/EU presence.
- **prepeat.io** – resolves to a site branded "ItaliaFire", an Italian
  restaurant/food-service platform.

## The near neighbour: PREPEAR

**Prepear, Inc.** (parent: Super Healthy Kids) makes a recipe and meal-planning
app – the same product in the same market, one letter away. They are known for
fighting Apple's opposition to their pear logo in 2020-21, so they engage with
trademark process rather than ignoring it.

Their registrations, all in **classes 42 and 45**:

| Office | Application | Filed |
|---|---|---|
| US (USPTO) | 87315341 | 2017-01-26 |
| UK (UKIPO) | UK00003212597 | 2017-02-14 |
| Canada | 1822836-00 | 2017-02-14 |
| Australia | 1825601 | 2017-02-14 |

**They have NO EU registration.** The only PREPEAR mark at EUIPO is
019070261 (filed 2024-08-22) owned by **PREPEAR Ltd** – a different business,
in classes 25, 32 and 43 (clothing, drinks, food services). That is the UK
"Prepear Drink Company" family, not the app.

So: nothing blocks Prep+Eat in the EU. The risk is that shipping worldwide on
the App Store makes the app visible in the US/UK where Prepear's class 42
covers software-as-a-service, and "Prepeat" vs "Prepear" in identical goods is
a real likelihood-of-confusion argument.

## The tagline is the bigger problem

"Prep. Eat. Repeat." is more encumbered than the name itself:

- **"Prep Eat Repeat"** – UK00004192070, filed 2025-04-21, **class 9**,
  **Registered**, Nicola Maria Georgiou. The exact phrase, in the exact class
  we would file in.
- **"PREP. EAT. REPEAT."** – **Sistema Plastics Limited** (the lunchbox brand),
  class 21, registered in New Zealand (1175963, 1175964) and as two WIPO
  international registrations (1628446, 1628447). Same phrase, same
  punctuation as ours.

**Open question for the attorney:** whether the Sistema WIPO registrations
designate the EU. International registrations name their designated countries;
that list was not readable from TMview's result view.

The good news is that a tagline is cheap to change and is not baked into the
bundle id, the domain or the wordmark. The name is the expensive thing to get
wrong, and the name is the part that is clear.

## Suggested next steps

1. **Treat the tagline as a separate decision from the name.** Using
   "Prep. Eat. Repeat." in App Store marketing copy is where the exposure sits.
2. **Optional, defensive: prepeat.dk.** We already own prepeat.app (the natural
   home for an app, and .app enforces HTTPS) and prepeat.love, so nothing is
   urgent. But .dk is the home market and costs little; .eu is more optional
   still. Note that owning a domain gives no trademark rights either way.
3. **If registering the mark:** an EU trade mark (EUTM) filed at EUIPO covers
   all 27 member states including Denmark in one application – roughly €850 for
   the first class, ~€50 for a second. Classes 9 and 42 would be the pair.
4. **Get an attorney's clearance** before filing, and certainly before any
   US/UK launch, given Prepear.
