# Installing Prep+Eat on your iPhone

You've been invited to try **Prep+Eat**, a meal-planning app for families.
It isn't in the App Store yet, so it's installed through Apple's testing
app, TestFlight. It's free, it's made by Apple, and it takes about five
minutes.

**Do all of this on your iPhone, not on a computer.** The invitation links
only work on the phone that will run the app.

---

## Step 1 – Install TestFlight

1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone
2. Search for **TestFlight**
3. Tap **Get** to install it (it's free, and it's by Apple)
4. Don't open it yet – just install it

---

## Step 2 – The first email

Look for an email with a subject like
**"You have been invited to join Sebell on App Store Connect"**.

It looks like a work invitation and it's full of unfamiliar words. Ignore
all that – it's just Apple's way of confirming who you are. You are not
signing up for anything, and it costs nothing.

1. Open the email **on your iPhone**
2. Tap **Accept invitation**
3. Sign in with your Apple ID – the same email address the invitation was
   sent to
4. Accept the terms when asked

**Don't skip this email.** If you go straight to the second one, it won't
work, and the error message won't explain why.

---

## Step 3 – The second email

A few minutes later you'll get a second email, this one from **TestFlight**,
saying you're invited to test Prep+Eat.

1. Open it **on your iPhone**
2. Tap **View in TestFlight**
3. Tap **Accept**
4. Tap **Install**

Prep+Eat now appears on your home screen, with a small orange dot next to
the name. That dot just means it's a test version.

---

## That's it

Open the app and sign in or create your account as normal.

When there's a new version, TestFlight sends you a notification. Open
TestFlight and tap **Update** next to Prep+Eat. You'll do this fairly often
while the app is being built.

---

## If something goes wrong

**"The invitation is no longer valid" or a similar error**
Almost always means Step 2 was skipped. Go back to the first email and
accept that invitation, then try the TestFlight email again.

**No emails arrived**
Check your spam or junk folder – Apple's invitations land there regularly.
If they're genuinely not there, the invitation may have gone to a different
email address. Tell Thomas which address you'd like it sent to.

**"This Apple ID is not associated with this invitation"**
The invitation went to one email address, but your iPhone is signed in with
a different Apple ID. Tell Thomas the address your iPhone actually uses –
you can find it in **Settings**, at the very top where your name is.

**You tapped the link and nothing sensible happened**
Usually TestFlight isn't installed yet. Do Step 1, then open the email
again.

**Still stuck**
Send Thomas a screenshot of what you're seeing. That's genuinely more
useful than describing it – the error messages are vague and a picture
usually shows the cause straight away.
