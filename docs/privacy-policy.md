# Prep+Eat – Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 27 July 2026**

Prep+Eat is a meal-planning app for families: you collect recipes, plan the
week's meals, and share a shopping list that updates on everyone's phone at
once. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and what you can do
about it.

It is written to match what the app actually does. If you find something here
that does not match your experience, that is a bug in one of the two – please
tell us.

## Who is responsible

Thomas Sebell, Denmark, is the data controller for Prep+Eat.

Contact: **thomas@sebell.dk**

## What we collect, and why

**To sign you in**
- Your **email address**. Sign-in works by sending a one-time code to your
  email; there is no password to store or lose.

**To make the app work as a shared family tool**
- Your **first name**, so the rest of your household can tell who added a
  recipe or ticked something off the list.
- Your **household**: its name, who belongs to it, and the invite codes used
  to join.
- Your **recipes**: titles, descriptions, ingredients, steps, serving counts,
  the web address you imported from (if any), and any **photos** you add. Each
  recipe records which member added it.
- Your **meal plans**: which meals are planned for which day, and for how many
  servings. Over time this builds a history of what your household eats.
- Your **shopping lists**: items, quantities, categories, and which member
  ticked an item off.
- **Learned categories**: when you move "Milk" to Dairy, the app remembers that
  for your household so it guesses better next time.

**To stop abuse**
- A record of **invite-code attempts** (which account tried, and when), so a
  code cannot be guessed by brute force.

**Automatically**
- Standard technical logs kept by our hosting provider, such as IP address and
  timestamps of requests. These are a by-product of running a server, not
  something the app reads or uses.

### What we do not collect

Prep+Eat contains **no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no
third-party marketing SDKs of any kind**. It does not ask for your location,
your contacts, or your health data, and it does not build an advertising
profile. It never sells or shares your data with anyone for advertising.

The only device permission the app asks for is access to your **photo library**,
and only when you choose to add a picture to a recipe.

### Our legal basis (GDPR)

- Providing the service you signed up for – planning meals and sharing lists
  with your household – is **performance of a contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)).
- Preventing abuse of invite codes is our **legitimate interest** in keeping
  households secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).

## What your household can see

This is the most important thing to understand about Prep+Eat: **a household
shares everything inside it.** Every member of your household can see the
household's recipes, meal plans and shopping lists, including your first name
against the recipes you added and your initial against the items you ticked
off.

Only invite the people you want to share a kitchen with.

## Who else handles your data

We use two companies to run the service. Both act on our instructions.

- **Supabase** – the database, sign-in, file storage and live sync behind the
  app. Your account, recipes, plans, lists and photos are stored there.
- **Apple** – distributes the app through the App Store and TestFlight, and
  delivers the sign-in emails you receive from us as ordinary email.

We do not use any other processors, and we do not transfer your data to anyone
else except where the law requires it.

## Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers operated by Supabase. Where those servers are
located outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is covered by the

## How long we keep it

- Your account data is kept for as long as your account exists.
- Items you delete inside the app – a recipe, a shopping item, a planned meal –
  are **hidden immediately but kept in the database for a short period** so
  that undo works and so a household member's deletion can be reversed. They
  are not visible to you or anyone else once deleted.
- If you **delete your profile**, your account and personal data are erased
  immediately and permanently. There is no grace period and no hidden copy.
  Recipes you added to a shared household stay with that household, but your
  name is removed from them.
- If you **leave a household**, you keep a copy of its recipes in a new
  personal kitchen, and the household keeps its own copy. The two are separate
  from that point on.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you,
- have inaccurate data **corrected**,
- have your data **erased** – the app does this for you under *Delete profile*,
- **restrict** or **object to** how we use your data,
- receive your data in a portable form.

Write to **thomas@sebell.dk** and we will answer within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the
Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500
Valby, Denmark – [datatilsynet.dk](https://www.datatilsynet.dk).

## Children

Prep+Eat is a family app, but accounts are meant for adults. We do not
knowingly create accounts for children under 13, and we do not target the app
at them. If a child has an account, a parent can delete it from inside the app
or by writing to us.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles your data, we will update this page and move
the "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
